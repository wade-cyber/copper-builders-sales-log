Weekly Consolidation

Runs automatically every Monday at 10:30 AM ET. Caches assignments, imports OSC leads from the Google Sheet into the database, consolidates all rep submissions into the weekly results, writes the results report to Google Sheets, and rotates the OSC Leads sheet for the next week.

Safe to rerun. Rerunning picks up any late submissions and reimports OSC lead data. It rebuilds from all current-week data and keeps each rep's latest entry per community — it will not duplicate or lose earlier data.

How the System Works

A guide for the admin dashboard manager. This page explains the weekly cycle, what each tab does, and how to handle common situations.

Weekly Cycle

Each week follows this cycle:

  1. Sales reps submit their weekly activity (leads, appointments, prospects, sales) through the Sales Log app at any time during the week.
  2. The OSC fills in the "This Week's Report" tab in the OSC Leads Report Google Sheet with marketing lead counts and VIP sign-ups for each community.
  3. Monday at 10:30 AM ET, the system automatically: caches assignments, imports OSC leads into the database, consolidates all submissions into the weekly results report, writes the report to Google Sheets, and rotates the OSC Leads sheet (archives the current week's tab and creates a fresh one for the next week).
  4. Results appear on the Results tab, organized by division and community, with summary cards, prospect pipeline, and last report times.

If a rep submits late or the OSC updates their numbers after Monday's consolidation, go to the System tab and click "Rerun Weekly Consolidation" — it will reimport OSC leads and pick up late submissions automatically. You can also click "Reimport OSC Leads" to reimport just the OSC data without rerunning the full consolidation.

ResultsView weekly results by community. Navigate between weeks with Prev/Next. Shows summary cards (VIP, OSC Leads, Rep Leads, Appts, Prospects, Sales), non-reporter alerts, the community breakdown table, prospect pipeline, and each rep's last submission date.

RepsAdd, activate, or deactivate sales reps.
AssignmentsAssign reps to communities for the current week. These determine which communities appear for each rep in the Sales Log app.

Common Tasks
SituationWhat to Do
A rep submitted lateGo to System tab → click "Rerun Weekly Consolidation". It rebuilds from all submissions and will include the late entry.
OSC leads not showing in ResultsOSC leads are imported automatically with the Monday consolidation. If they're missing, go to System tab → click "Reimport OSC Leads". Make sure the OSC has filled in the "This Week's Report" tab first.
Add a new communityCommunities tab → enter name and division → click Add Community. Then assign reps on the Assignments tab.
Add a new sales repReps tab → enter name → click Add Rep. Then assign their communities on the Assignments tab.
Rep can't see their communitiesCheck the Assignments tab — the rep must be assigned to the community for the current week.
Monday consolidation failedCheck System tab for error details. Most common cause: Google Sheets API quota exceeded. Wait a few minutes and click "Rerun Weekly Consolidation".
Need to see a previous weekResults tab → use the Prev/Next buttons to navigate between weeks.
