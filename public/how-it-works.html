How It Works

Copper Builders — Weekly Sales Log

This app is where sales reps submit their weekly activity report. It takes about 2 minutes to fill out each week.

Watch the Walkthrough

What You'll Report Each Week

For each project you're assigned to, you'll enter:

New Leads

How many new leads did you receive directly this week? Enter counts for:

  • Digital — website inquiries, email, online forms
  • Phone Call — inbound phone calls
  • In Person — walk-ins, open house visitors
Appointments

How many appointments did you hold this week?

  • Virtual — phone calls, video meetings
  • In Person — on-site visits, model tours
Prospects / Sales

Track your active buyers and log sales:

  • Add a prospect — enter their name, ranking (A/B/C), and optional lot number
  • Mark as Sold — click "Sold" next to a prospect to log a sale for the week
  • Remove — click "Remove" if a prospect is no longer active

Prospects carry over week to week. Sold prospects are logged as sales and removed from your active list.

Step by Step

1
Open the app — go to copper-builders-log.vercel.app on your phone or computer
2
Select your name from the dropdown. Your assigned projects will load automatically.
3
Tap a project to expand it. Fill in your leads, appointments, and prospect updates for that project.
4
Repeat for each of your assigned projects. The progress bar at the top tracks how many you've reviewed.
5
Click "Submit Weekly Log" at the bottom. You'll see a confirmation with your totals.
Deadline: Reports are due every Monday by 10:00 AM. You can submit anytime during the week — the report covers activity through the previous Sunday.

What Happens to Your Data

After you submit:

  • Your data is saved immediately to Google Sheets
  • On Monday at 10:30 AM, all reports are automatically consolidated into a weekly summary
  • Your sales manager can see who submitted and who didn't
  • If you need to update your report, just select your name and submit again — the latest submission is what counts

Ranking Guide

Each prospect is rated on whether they are Ready, Willing, and Able to buy. The ranking reflects how many of those three boxes they check.

RankTemperatureCriteriaWhen to Use
AHotReady, Willing, and Able — all 3Actively engaged, qualified buyer, likely to close soon
BWarm2 of the 3 aboveInterested and mostly qualified, but one factor is missing — needs follow-up
CCold1 of the 3 aboveEarly stage, browsing, unqualified, or long timeline

Tips

  • You can submit from your phone, tablet, or computer — just open the link in any browser
  • If you enter zeros for a project, that's fine — it just means no activity that week
  • Prospects stay in your list until you mark them Sold or Remove them
  • If you don't see a project you're working on, let your sales manager know — they manage the assignment list

Questions?

Contact your sales manager if you have issues with assignments, missing projects, or need help using the app.
